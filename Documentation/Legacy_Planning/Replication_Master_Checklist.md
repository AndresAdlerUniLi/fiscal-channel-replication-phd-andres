# Replication Master Checklist

## Paper
Title: The Fiscal Channel of Monetary Policy  
Authors: Max Breitenlechner, Martin Geiger, Mathias Klein  

---

## Main Objective
Replicate the empirical framework used to study how fiscal policy shapes the transmission of monetary policy shocks.
